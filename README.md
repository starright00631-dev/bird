# Bird–Glass Collision Research

Research-grade, fixed-building mechanistic modelling of bird flight near transparent and reflective facades, with multi-pane collision evaluation and uncertainty-aware intervention analysis.

**Scientific status:** Structural simulation and externally sourced observational baselines, **not** a calibrated prediction system or a site-approved intervention design. Published animal-flight parameters must not be assumed to transfer to unmeasured species and buildings.

## Research workflow

1. Track motion, geometry, optical ambiguity, and observation effort separately.
2. Fit perception and control parameters against independent real trajectories and avoided approaches.
3. Evaluate joint-facade counterfactuals, including risk transferred to adjacent glass or opaque walls.
4. Require measured site geometry and credible field validation before recommending installation.

No glass replacement is considered. Source data licenses and contributor privacy must be checked before sharing raw records.
