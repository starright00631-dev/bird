# BNBU local bird-collision records: audit boundary

**Status:** aggregate-only local context; not a representative training dataset.

The supplied BNBU survey material contains 48 submissions, 47 collision reports after excluding one near-miss report, and 50 reported birds. At least two records are marked as suspected rather than fully confirmed. The 38-entry microzone catalogue is a localization and field-audit queue, not a verified list of impacted panes.

## What these records can support

- Candidate species names and guild hypotheses to cross-check against regional occurrence data.
- Reported dates, broad campus areas and incident descriptions for planning field verification.
- A list of locations and façade questions to prioritize for photographs and standardized surveys.
- Qualitative checks on whether external species/season hypotheses are locally plausible.

## What they cannot identify

- A collision rate per pane, façade-hour or bird approach.
- A surveyed negative or a true zero where no report exists.
- A complete species-abundance or seasonal migration profile.
- A carcass persistence or observer-detection correction.
- A causal effect of a glass type, vegetation intervention or retrofit.

The reports are opportunistic and do not provide a standardized search schedule, complete approach denominator, consistent pane IDs or verified impact surfaces. A missing report must never be converted into a negative training row. Raw submissions containing submitter identifiers are not committed.

## How the project should use them

Keep the local records in a restricted source file and expose only anonymous aggregates to the public repository. Use them for hypothesis generation and field localization. Calibrate collision rates only after a repeated survey design records both positive and explicit zero outcomes with effort, façade, date, detection and persistence information.
