# BNBU local-record audit and field-verification boundary

**Status: aggregate audit only.** The original local submission sheet contains respondent-identifying fields and is not copied into this public repository.

## Current source facts

- 48 submissions in the supplied BNBU campus form.
- 47 reports describe a collision after excluding one clear near-miss/avoidance record.
- 50 birds are reported across those collision reports.
- At least two records are explicitly marked as suspected rather than confirmed.
- The current microzone verification catalogue contains 38 candidate areas.

These are report counts, not incidence rates. A report may describe multiple birds; a bird count is not an event count; and an event report is not automatically a verified glass impact.

## Spatial resolution

The 38 microzone entries are a **field-verification queue**, not 38 validated collision panes. At the time of the source audit:

- no candidate zone had verified pane geometry;
- no candidate zone had an identified impact surface;
- the catalogue did not contain reliable pane IDs, full pane dimensions, glass build, surface number, orientation or an exposure denominator.

T29 appears in 13 location descriptions, but these refer to different settings (bridge/railing, activity area, first-floor glass, terraces/platforms and connecting areas). They must not be collapsed into one “T29 window” or treated as one continuous glass surface. LRC descriptions also mix a building-level reference, entrance, side, slope and separate glass railings.

A map centroid or building POI is not a glass coordinate. A discovery point is not necessarily the impact point; the report describing an upper-level pane found at ground level is an explicit example of this distinction.

## Identification and time uncertainty

Species names include unknown, uncertain, colloquial and non-species-level identifications. Event time and submission time are not interchangeable; at least one source row has an event/submission timestamp inconsistency. Some reports may also describe multiple birds or closely timed events that require follow-up rather than automatic deduplication.

The month distribution cannot be interpreted as seasonal collision risk because the source has no known survey-effort denominator by month, building or pane. A month with no submission is not a month with zero collisions.

## What these records can support now

1. Create a ranked field-verification queue for candidate buildings and microzones.
2. Identify recurring location descriptions that need disambiguation.
3. Form hypotheses about glass, vegetation, water, corridors and time for targeted inspection.
4. Check whether external studies and intervention mechanisms are plausible in the BNBU context.
5. Link a future verified event to a photographed and measured pane.

## What these records cannot support now

- A pane-level collision probability or defensible building risk ranking.
- A species susceptibility classifier.
- A seasonal/monthly collision-rate model.
- A causal estimate of treatment efficacy or a BNBU percentage reduction.
- Negative labels from buildings/panes with no submitted report.
- Tree removal, pruning or habitat removal based on report density or canopy percentage alone.

## Required field verification for each candidate pane

Record the following with stable IDs and provenance:

- pane/facade ID, georeferenced position, building, face, floor and structure type;
- width, height, area, orientation, height above ground and adjacent opaque structures;
- complete glazing assembly, tint/low-E coating and coating position, exterior VLR/VLT if measured, and which surface carries any treatment;
- exterior photographs from plausible bird approach angles at multiple solar times and sky conditions;
- pattern coverage, surface, maximum clear gaps, component geometry/area and contrast;
- vegetation cover at 0–5 m, 5–25 m, 25–100 m, 100 m–1 km and 1–10 km, plus nearest crown, canopy height, pane-height overlap and measured reflected vegetation;
- local bird activity with declared survey effort and taxonomic confidence;
- continuous video or another valid sampling design for all approaches through a pane-specific risk gate, including glass contact, opaque contact, no contact and right-censored events;
- survey effort, searcher efficiency and carcass persistence if ground searches are used.

## Data governance

Do not commit respondent names, email addresses, student identifiers, exact private contact details, unredacted incident descriptions or original restricted spreadsheets. Keep only aggregate audit facts and non-identifying derived variables in the public repository.

**Model rule:** the 48 submissions are a localization and hypothesis source. External, effort-aware datasets remain the primary basis for model construction until comparable BNBU exposure and outcome data are collected.
